2Research

Adversarial Collaboration Between AI Agents Under Uncertainty

Independent research preprint · May 2026 · Multi-agent AI, reliability

I investigated whether three AI agents working as a generator, adversary, and independent verifier can improve reasoning reliability. The adversary challenges an answer, but the generator revises it only if the verifier confirms a real error. In the reported reasoning-task experiments, this approach reached 87% accuracy, compared with 50% for a single agent and 62% for self-refinement. The paper discusses the higher computational cost and limitations of its small-scale evaluation.

Can LLMs Recognize Their Own Knowledge Boundaries?

Archit Ranjan and Adit Maheshwari · Research preprint · May 2026 · LLM evaluation, uncertainty, hallucinations

We studied whether large language models know when to answer, ask for clarification, or abstain. Testing an open-weight model on 728 responses across 722 unique question IDs, we observed 80.2% overall behavioral accuracy, but only 32.7% on ambiguous prompts and 70.4% on questions with false premises. The findings suggest that identifying missing context or invalid assumptions is harder than recognizing obviously unknowable information. The study evaluates one model and uses self-reported confidence.
