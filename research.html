1Adversarial Collaboration Between AI Agents Under Uncertainty

Archit Ranjan · Independent researcher · May 2026 · Preprint

1.1Summary

This preprint examines when collaborating AI agents produce more reliable answers than a single model. It presents a closed-loop system with three roles: a generator proposes a response, an adversary tries to find mistakes, and an independent verifier determines whether the criticism is valid. Only verified failures trigger a revision.

The paper reports 87% accuracy for adversarial validation on its reasoning tasks, versus 50% for the single-agent baseline and 62% for self-refinement. The author notes that the evaluation is relatively small and that the method increases cost and latency, with its effectiveness dependent on reliable verification.
